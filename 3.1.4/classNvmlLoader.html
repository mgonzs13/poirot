Detailed Description

Minimal runtime loader for the NVIDIA Management Library.

Loads libnvidia-ml.so.1 with dlopen and resolves only the symbols POIROT needs. No headers or build-time NVML dependency are required. The library is intentionally never closed and nvmlShutdown() is never called because NVML is process-wide and may be shared with other components.
